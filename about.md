# Nater's Tournament

Live 1v1 Geometry Dash tournaments, made for Nater's streams.

- Browse public tournaments, or join a private one with its code.
- Players drop into the lobby, and the host starts the bracket.
- Matches race both players on the same level, with your opponent's live progress at the top of the screen.
- Watch the bracket map as players clash and advance.

Emotes are Nater's own Timer Battles emotes.
